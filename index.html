MOBA-FIT — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 September 2026

MOBA-FIT is a multiplayer virtual reality game for Meta Horizon devices. This policy explains what the game collects, why, who it goes to, and how to have it deleted. It is written to be read rather than to cover us, so it describes what the software actually does.

1. What we collect

DataWhere it comes fromWhy
Your Meta user ID The Meta Horizon platform, when you launch the game To tell players apart within a match, to return you to your own team slot if you disconnect and come back, and to serve as a stable identity for future features such as a ranked ladder.
Your Meta username (your handle) The Meta Horizon platform Shown to the other players in your match: on the lobby roster, the team board, and the nameplate above your character, so people can tell who is who.
A name you type yourself (optional) You, on the in-game keyboard Used instead of your Meta username if you choose to set one. Stored only on your own headset.
In-match position and movement Your headset, while a match is running So the other players can see where your character is. This is the ordinary traffic of any multiplayer game.
Voice audio, while you are speaking Your headset microphone, only when voice chat is active Relayed live so you can talk to your team. It is passed straight through and is never recorded or stored. See the note below on who can receive it.
Your IP address The network connection itself Unavoidable in order to send data back to your headset. It appears in the game server's operational logs.
We do not collect your real name. Meta offers apps both a display name and a username. MOBA-FIT deliberately reads only the username — the handle you chose — and never the display name, which on many accounts is a person's legal name. There is no fallback: if you have no username set, the game shows the name you typed, or a placeholder.

We do not collect your email address, your friends list, your date of birth, your payment details, or any camera, hand-tracking, or room-scanning data. The game shows no advertising and contains no advertising or analytics software.

2. How your data is used

Everything listed above is used for one purpose: running the multiplayer game you chose to play. Specifically, to place you in a match, to show you to the other players, to let you talk to your team, and to keep the game's state consistent between the headsets taking part.

We do not use your data for advertising or marketing. We do not build a profile of you. We do not sell, rent, or trade your data to anyone, and we will not do so in the future.

3. Who it is shared with

The other players in your match. Your username and your character's position are shown to the other people playing with you. That is the point of a multiplayer game.

About voice chat, stated plainly. Voice is intended for your own team, and the game plays it only to your teammates. However, for technical reasons the audio is currently delivered to every player in the match and it is each player's copy of the game that discards it if they are not on your team. This means a player running a modified copy of the game could listen to your team's voice chat. We think you should know that rather than discover it. If this matters to you, do not say anything over voice chat that you would not say to everyone in the match. We intend to fix this by filtering voice on the server instead.

Our game server. Match traffic passes through a server we rent in order to connect players to one another. It is operated on our behalf by:

ProviderRoleLocation
Hetzner Online GmbH Hosting provider for the game server. They supply the machine; they do not use the data for their own purposes. Server located in Oregon, United States. Hetzner is headquartered in Germany.

Nobody else. There are no analytics providers, no advertising networks, and no data brokers involved in this game.

4. How long we keep it

Your username, your position, and your voice exist only for the duration of the match and are held in the server's memory, not written to disk. Voice audio is forwarded and immediately dropped; no copy is kept anywhere. When the match ends or you disconnect, all of it is gone.

Two things persist longer:

  • A saved world file on the game server records a numeric player identifier (derived from your Meta user ID) alongside the last position of your character, so that you return to where you left off. It contains no name and no other personal information.
  • Server operational logs record connection events, including IP addresses. They are kept for up to 30 days for debugging and abuse prevention, and are then deleted.

Any name you typed yourself is stored on your own headset only, and is removed when you uninstall the game.

5. Deleting your data

You can ask us to delete your data at any time, wherever in the world you live, and it is free. We will never charge a fee for a deletion request.

Email binarytendermusic@gmail.com with the subject line Delete my data, telling us the Meta username you play under. We will delete everything associated with you and confirm by reply within 30 days.

You may also ask what data we hold about you, or ask us to correct it, using the same address. You do not need to give a reason for any of these requests.

6. Children

MOBA-FIT is rated for teens and adults (13+, or the applicable age in your region) and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with data, contact us at the address above and we will delete it.

7. Security

Match traffic travels between your headset and our server. The server accepts messages only from players holding a valid session, and rejects malformed or out-of-range messages. Administrative access to the server itself is restricted to the developer by key-based authentication.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects what we collect or who we share it with, we will update the date at the top of this page and, where the change is significant, tell players inside the game. This page is always the current version.
